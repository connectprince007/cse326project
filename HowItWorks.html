Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about this academic prototype.

Is this a real rental service?

No. RideNest is a student project. It does not reserve real vehicles or collect payments.

Can I compare different vehicle types?

Yes. Open the Vehicle Information page and compare cars, bikes, and scooters.

Where can I see my booking?

Demo bookings can be stored in your browser when the booking functionality is implemented.

What documents are required?

A real rental provider may require valid identification, a driving licence, and a deposit.
